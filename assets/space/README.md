# Orbital setting

`earth-june-5400.jpg` is the 5400×2700 June 2004 Blue Marble: Next Generation base map from NASA Earth Observatory (Reto Stöckli and the Blue Marble team).

- Source page: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/
- Original download: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/june/world.200406.3x5400x2700.jpg
- Retrieved 2026-09-28. Original JPEG retained; used as a local globe texture.

The procedural clouds, atmosphere, star field and carrier geometry are original presentation elements in `scripts/web/space-environment.js` and `scripts/web/carrier-exterior.js`. The carrier uses tapered faceted armor, a recessed launch collar, thin launch rails, swept nacelles and layered aft engines; its 3,528 triangles are grouped into eight material batches. The carrier is an original design around the existing maintenance bay, not a replica of a named Gundam ship. Planet and ship distances are composed for the scene rather than astronomical scale.
